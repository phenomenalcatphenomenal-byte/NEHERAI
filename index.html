NEHORAI (NEHO)

The Utility Token for an Intelligent Digital Economy

NEHORAI is a technology-driven ecosystem focused on building intelligent digital infrastructure through Artificial Intelligence (AI), blockchain technology, and decentralized innovation. Its native utility token, NEHO, powers participation, access, incentives, and value exchange across the ecosystem.

The NEHORAI Solution

NEHORAI will develop a suite of interconnected digital products that leverage AI and blockchain technology to solve practical problems. The ecosystem is modular, allowing products to evolve independently while remaining connected through the NEHO token.

AI Tools & Automation

Intelligent solutions for productivity, content, analytics, and workflow optimization.

Decentralized Infrastructure

Scalable blockchain-based systems for secure and transparent digital interactions.

Community Governance

Mechanisms that allow token holders to participate in ecosystem decisions over time.

Vision & Mission

To build an intelligent digital economy where technology empowers people, connects communities, and creates sustainable opportunities globally. NEHORAI's mission is to develop accessible and scalable AI and blockchain-powered infrastructure that delivers real-world utility while fostering innovation, transparency, and community participation.

Roadmap Phases: Foundation, Development, & Product Launch.

NEHORAI's roadmap is structured to deliver tangible value through iterative development and community involvement.
Launch NEHO on Solana via Pump.fun
Research AI-powered utilities & decentralized infrastructure
Launch first utility product & integrate NEHO as payment token
Check the official channels or consult the NEHORAI team for the latest roadmap updates.
Q3 2026
Phase 1: Foundation
Launch NEHO on Solana via Pump.fun. Establish brand identity, publish whitepaper, build community on X and Telegram. Begin strategic planning for ecosystem utilities.
Q4 2026
Phase 2: Ecosystem Development
Research and prototype AI-powered utility tools. Develop decentralized infrastructure concepts. Establish partnerships with developers and technology providers. Expand community engagement.
Q1 2027
Phase 3: Product Launch
Launch the first NEHORAI utility product. Integrate NEHO as a payment and access token. Release developer resources. Begin testing staking and reward mechanisms.
